Finding your way to us

Getting here

Visakhapatnam is well connected by air and rail, and the resort sits a short drive up the coast road.

By air

Visakhapatnam Airport

VTZ is roughly 40 minutes from the resort. Direct flights from Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Delhi and Mumbai.

By rail

Visakhapatnam Junction

About 30 minutes away, on the Howrah–Chennai line with frequent services.

Come dressed for the coast

What to wear

December on the Bay of Bengal is warm by day and breezy after sunset. Light fabrics, and something you can walk on sand in.

Colours we love

Sea glass, sand, coral, ivory and gold — anything that catches the light off the water.

A gentle note

Please avoid stilettos for the beachside functions — the sand will win. Flats, wedges or bare feet are perfect. Carry a light shawl for the evening breeze.
